User Guide

Dashboard layout

The dashboard has three areas:

  • Toplists (left): Gainers, Gainers Open and Volume Leaders.
  • Alerts (top right): New HoD, Buying Pressure and Selling Pressure.
  • Bottom row: Fast-Growing Momentum with its chart, the Charts panel (Bull vs. Bear, Key levels and Rally tracker for one ticker), and Halts.

The chip in the top bar shows the market session in New York time. Its dot pulses while a trading session is running.

Market sessions
  • Pre-Market: 4:00 – 9:30 ET
  • Market Open: 9:30 – 16:00 ET
  • After Hours: 16:00 – 20:00 ET
  • Closed: outside those hours and on weekends
Toplists
  • Gainers: biggest % change from the previous close.
  • Gainers Open: biggest % change from today's open. Shown only during Market Open (hidden in Pre-Market, After Hours and Closed); Gainers and Volume Leaders take its space.
  • Volume Leaders: highest volume traded today.

Prices update live and flash green when they rise, red when they fall.

Alert tables
  • New HoD: a ticker just made a new high of the day.
  • Buying / Selling Pressure: strong one-sided order flow.
  • Fast-Growing Momentum: fast gains over 1, 5, 15 and 30 minutes.

New alerts appear at the top. The speaker icon in a table's bar sets its alert sound.

Momentum constellation

The chart above the Fast-Growing Momentum table answers "who has momentum right now?". Each ticker in the table is a label: the bigger, brighter, warmer and closer to the center, the more momentum.

  • Every alert adds heat, which fades with a 4-minute half-life. A ticker leaves 15 minutes after its last alert.
  • ▲: an alert in the last minute. A halted ticker shows a pause icon and HALT with its duration; its heat stays frozen until it resumes.
  • It follows the table: excluded tickers and active filters apply here too.
  • Hover a ticker for its details; click it to select it.
Charts panel

Three views of one ticker: Bull vs. Bear, Key levels and Rally tracker. The tabs switch the view; the arrow keys move between them.

  • The fullscreen icon opens the panel in fullscreen. There, the layout icon shows one, two or three charts at once, side by side (vertical), stacked (horizontal) or mixed.
  • With several charts, click one to make it active (green edge): the tabs change the active chart. Picking a view already on screen swaps the two.
  • Drag the gap between two charts to resize them; double-click it to split evenly again. The layout and sizes are remembered.
Halts

Trading pauses (for example LULD halts). Duration counts the time since the halt and Resume Est. counts down to the expected reopening. A halt leaves the table once trading resumes.

Resizing panels
  • Drag the lines between panels to resize them.
  • With a line focused, use the arrow keys (hold Shift for bigger steps).
  • Double-click a line to restore the default size.

Your layout is saved in this browser.

Columns order

Drag a column header to move it. The first columns (Signal, Time, Ticker) can't be moved. When you scroll sideways, Signal stays put, Time scrolls away and Ticker sticks next to Signal. Each table remembers its own order.

Drag the right edge of a header to resize its column, or double-click the edge to fit the content. Each table remembers its own widths (a detached copy keeps its own); Reset widths in Table settings brings back the defaults.

Table settings

The sliders icon in a table's bar opens its settings. Nothing changes until you press Save changes.

  • Columns: drag to reorder, switch columns on or off, and pick the color of Vol. 1m and Hits. Reset restores the defaults.
  • Exclude tickers: search a ticker and exclude it from the table. Tick all tables to exclude it everywhere.
  • Float tiers: the colored dots on the Signal (or Float) row open them. Set where Low, Mid and High float start and their colors; they apply to every table.
Alert sound

The speaker icon in an alert table's bar opens its sound settings. It lights up while that table's sound is on.

  • Voice reads each new ticker, Chime plays a short tone, Custom plays your own audio file.
  • Set the volume and press Play preview to try it. Switch tables with the chips at the top.
Fullscreen
  • The icon in a table's bar (or in the Charts panel) opens it in fullscreen.
  • The icon in the top bar opens the whole dashboard in fullscreen.
  • Press Esc or the same icon to exit.
On your phone

The tab strip at the top switches between the tables and the Charts panel (Gainers Open shows up only during Market Open). A number on a tab counts the alerts that arrived since you last looked at it.

  • Toplists keep a compact table: swipe it sideways for more columns.
  • Alert tables show their rows as cards. When new alerts arrive while you are scrolled down, tap the “new” pill to jump back to the top.

Table settings, filters, alert sounds, fullscreen and detached copies are set up on desktop.
