User Guide

Dashboard layout

The dashboard has three areas:

  • Toplists (left): Gainers, Gainers Open and Volume Leaders.
  • Alerts (top right): New HoD, Buying Pressure and Selling Pressure.
  • Bottom row: Fast-Growing Momentum with its chart, a second chart, and Halts.

The chip in the top bar shows the market session in New York time. Its dot pulses while a trading session is running.

Market sessions
  • Pre-Market: 4:00 – 9:30 ET
  • Market Open: 9:30 – 16:00 ET
  • After Hours: 16:00 – 20:00 ET
  • Closed: outside those hours and on weekends
Toplists
  • Gainers: biggest % change from the previous close.
  • Gainers Open: biggest % change from today's open.
  • Volume Leaders: highest volume traded today.

Prices update live and flash green when they rise, red when they fall.

Alert tables
  • New HoD: a ticker just made a new high of the day.
  • Buying / Selling Pressure: strong one-sided order flow.
  • Fast-Growing Momentum: fast gains over 1, 5, 15 and 30 minutes.

New alerts appear at the top. Turn on the speaker icon in a table's bar to hear a sound for each new alert.

Halts

Trading pauses (for example LULD halts). Duration counts the time since the halt and Resume Est. counts down to the expected reopening. A halt leaves the table once trading resumes.

Resizing panels
  • Drag the lines between panels to resize them.
  • With a line focused, use the arrow keys (hold Shift for bigger steps).
  • Double-click a line to restore the default size.

Your layout is saved in this browser.

Columns order

Drag a column header to move it. The first columns (Signal, Time, Ticker) can't be moved. When you scroll sideways, Signal stays put, Time scrolls away and Ticker sticks next to Signal. Each table remembers its own order.

Table settings

The sliders icon in a table's bar opens its settings. Nothing changes until you press Save changes.

  • Columns: drag to reorder, switch columns on or off, and pick the color of Vol. 1m and Hits. Reset restores the defaults.
  • Exclude tickers: search a ticker and exclude it from the table. Tick all tables to exclude it everywhere.
  • Float tiers: the colored dots on the Signal (or Float) row open them. Set where Low, Mid and High float start and their colors; they apply to every table.
Fullscreen
  • The icon in a table's bar opens that table in fullscreen.
  • The icon in the top bar opens the whole dashboard in fullscreen.
  • Press Esc or the same icon to exit.
On your phone

Panels are stacked and each table shows its rows as cards. When new alerts arrive while you are scrolled down, tap the “new” pill to jump back to the top.
